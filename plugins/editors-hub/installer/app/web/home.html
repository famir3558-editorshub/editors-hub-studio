Your videos

Projects

One project per client: their details, brandbook, brand guidelines and videos. Pick any style when you make a video, and the client's brand stays separate from it.

My styles

Reusable editing looks, with their rules (STYLE.md), references and the videos that train them. Use any style for any client. Your styles are private: only you see them until you share one.

Recently deleted

Deleted clients and styles wait here with their videos and files. Restore puts them back. Delete forever erases them for good.

Shared

Styles shared with everyone in the studio. Anyone who makes videos can use them; only the person who shared a style, or the owner, can unshare it.

Online shared folder

Styles uploaded online can be downloaded into Editors.Hub on any of your computers.

Editors.Hub installs what your edits need on this computer, in this order. It carries on by itself; a few steps need you for a moment.

Team

Everyone's sign-up and sign-in code comes to you. Give a code only to people you want in.

Waiting for a code

Members

Editors make styles and videos, upload drafts and leave notes. Reviewers (for clients) watch, leave notes, approve or ask for changes. Only you, the owner, can do admin things.

Email the codes to you

So codes reach your inbox, Editors.Hub sends them from a Gmail account. Use a Gmail app password, not your normal password: Google Account → Security → 2-Step Verification → App passwords → create one for "Editors.Hub" and paste the 16 letters here. It's kept only on this computer.

Settings

API keys

The services your edits can use. Paste a key yourself here (never into a chat). Keys stay on this computer, in a file only you can read, outside the studio's folder, so copying the studio never carries them.

Skills that come with Editors.Hub

These Claude skills are packed inside the studio. Whenever Editors.Hub starts on a computer, it installs any that are missing and updates the ones nobody has changed. A skill you've set up (like your brand from creator-setup) or that came from somewhere else is never overwritten.
